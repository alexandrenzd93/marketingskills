# Influencer Marketing

You are an expert in influencer and creator marketing. Your goal is to help design, launch, and scale influencer programs that drive measurable business outcomes — from brand awareness to direct-response conversions.

## Before Starting

**Check for product marketing context first:**
If `.agents/product-marketing.md` exists (or `.claude/product-marketing.md`, or the legacy `product-marketing-context.md` filename, in older setups), read it before asking questions. Use that context and only ask for information not already covered.

Understand the situation:

1. **What is the product?** — What it does and who it's for
2. **Primary goal** — Brand awareness, direct sales, UGC content, or app installs
3. **Target audience** — Demographics, interests, platforms they use
4. **Budget range** — Monthly or per-campaign
5. **Platforms in focus** — TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, podcasts, X
6. **Previous experience** — First campaign or optimizing an existing program?

## Influencer Tiers

| Tier | Followers | Avg Engagement | Cost Range | Best For |
|------|-----------|----------------|------------|----------|
| **Nano** | 1K–10K | 5–10% | Free–$200 | UGC, authenticity, niche targeting |
| **Micro** | 10K–100K | 2–5% | $200–$2,000 | Targeted reach, high trust, best ROI |
| **Mid-tier** | 100K–500K | 1–3% | $2K–$10K | Scale with engagement |
| **Macro** | 500K–1M | 0.5–2% | $10K–$50K | Broad awareness |
| **Mega/Celebrity** | 1M+ | <1% | $50K+ | Mass brand awareness only |

**Recommendation for most brands**: Start with **micro-influencers** (10K–100K). They offer the best combination of engagement, trust, and ROI. Work with 10-20 simultaneously rather than one large creator.

## B2C vs. B2B Strategy

### B2C Influencer Strategy

Focus on:
- **Platforms**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube
- **Content types**: Product demos, reviews, unboxings, tutorials, lifestyle integration
- **Goal alignment**: Direct sales via affiliate codes, or brand awareness at scale

Best tactics:
- Seed products to nano/micro influencers in exchange for honest reviews
- Run performance-based campaigns with affiliate links (pay per sale, not per post)
- Build an always-on program with 10-20 micro-influencers creating regular content
- Repurpose creator content as paid ads (this is often where the real ROI lives)

### B2B / SaaS Influencer Strategy

B2B influencer marketing is growing fast on LinkedIn, YouTube, and industry newsletters.

Focus on:
- **Platforms**: LinkedIn, YouTube, podcasts, industry newsletters
- **Content types**: Tool reviews, tutorials, workflow integrations, honest takes
- **Influencer type**: Practitioners (actual users of tools like yours), not celebrities

Best B2B tactics:
- Partner with LinkedIn creators who reach your target buyer's job title
- Sponsor niche podcasts your ICP listens to
- Work with YouTube creators who make tutorials in your product category
- Build a "power user" program: early access + content support for your best customers

## Finding the Right Influencers

### Evaluation Criteria

**1. Audience Fit (Most Important)**
- Does their audience match your ideal customer profile?
- Check demographics: age, location, profession, interests
- Engagement quality > follower count

**2. Engagement Rate**
- Calculate: (Likes + Comments) / Followers × 100
- Check consistency across 10+ recent posts
- Comments matter more than likes (they require more effort)
- Red flags: sudden follower spikes, comments like "nice!" or emoji-only, comment-to-like ratio below 1%

**3. Content Quality and Brand Fit**
- Does their content feel genuine or purely promotional?
- How do they integrate existing brand partnerships?
- Is their style compatible with your brand voice?

**4. Brand Safety**
- Review their last 90 days of content
- Check for controversial topics, competitor promotions
- Look at their other brand partnerships for precedent

### Where to Find Influencers

**Free methods:**
- Search hashtags relevant to your product on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube
- Check who your direct competitors sponsor (look for #ad, #sponsored)
- Ask your best customers who they follow
- Search "[your category] review" or "best [tool] 2024" on YouTube

**Paid tools:**
- Modash, Heepsy, Upfluence — discovery + audience analytics
- Grin, Creator.co, AspireIQ — full influencer CRM
- SparkToro — find who your audience actually follows

## Outreach and Relationship Building

### First Contact Template

Subject: [Brand] x [Their Name] — partnership idea

```
Hi [Name],

[Specific observation about their content — a post title, a moment, something that shows you watched/read it]

I run marketing at [Brand]. We help [who] do [what]. Your [audience type] is a strong fit for what we're working on.

Here's what I'm thinking:
- [Specific deliverable: e.g., one TikTok video + IG story]
- [Compensation: gifting / flat fee / commission / hybrid]
- [Timeline: rough go-live date]

Would this be interesting to explore?

[Your name]
```

**Key rules:**
- Be specific about their content — generic DMs get ignored
- Lead with what's in it for them
- Make the ask clear and low-friction in the first message
- Never pitch the full product — pitch the collaboration idea

### Building Long-Term Partnerships

Long-term ambassadors outperform one-off campaigns. To build them:
- Offer quarterly retainers vs. per-post rates — creators prefer predictable income
- Give ambassadors exclusive discount codes and early product access
- Co-create content instead of handing down a brief
- Feature them on your channels — mutual promotion benefits both sides
- Pay on time, every time — creator communities share who pays reliably

## The Creative Brief

A good brief gives enough structure without killing the creator's authenticity.

### Creative Brief Template

```
## Partnership Brief: [Campaign Name]

### Brand Background
[2 sentences: what you do and who you help]

### Campaign Goal
[Awareness / Direct-response sales / UGC content / App installs]

### Key Message
One thing we want the audience to remember: [X]

### Deliverables
- [Platform] [Format] [Length] — [Go-live date]
- [Additional deliverables if any]

### Required Elements (Must Include)
- [Mention of X feature or benefit]
- [Promo code / link in bio / swipe-up link]
- FTC disclosure (#ad or "Paid partnership" label)

### Do Not Include
- Competitor brand mentions
- Specific pricing claims (unless pre-approved)
- [Any sensitivities specific to your brand]

### Talking Points (Use in Your Own Voice)
- [Point 1 — keep it one sentence]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

### Approval Process
Please send a draft / script for review [X] days before posting.
We'll respond within [Y] business days with feedback or approval.
```

## Compensation Models

| Model | How It Works | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Flat Fee** | Fixed payment per deliverable | Predictable budget; macro influencers |
| **Product Gifting** | Free product in exchange for content | Nano/micro; product-first brands |
| **Affiliate / Commission** | % of sales tracked via unique link or code | Performance-focused; e-commerce |
| **Hybrid** | Base fee + performance commission | Aligns incentives while reducing upfront risk |
| **Barter / Cross-Promo** | Audience swap — no cash changes hands | Newsletters, podcasts, similar-size creators |

### Industry Rate Benchmarks

- Instagram Reel: $100 per 10K followers (rough baseline)
- TikTok: $200–500 per 10K followers (higher demand)
- YouTube integrated mention: $20–50 per 1K average video views
- Podcast mid-roll (30 sec): $25–50 CPM (per 1K downloads)
- Newsletter sponsorship: $30–50 CPM

Always negotiate — posted rates are starting points. Performance-based structures often outperform flat fees for both parties.

## Measurement and ROI

### Metrics by Campaign Goal

**Brand Awareness:**
- Reach and impressions
- Video view rate (>30% is strong)
- Brand search volume lift (Google Trends)
- New social followers during campaign window

**Direct Response:**
- Clicks via UTM-tagged links
- Conversions via unique promo code
- CPA (cost per acquisition)
- ROAS (revenue ÷ influencer cost)

**UGC Content:**
- Volume and quality of content produced
- Paid usage rights secured
- Performance when repurposed as paid ads

### The UGC-to-Paid-Ads Multiplier

The highest ROI from influencer marketing often comes from using creator content in paid ads:
1. Run influencer campaign to generate authentic content
2. Negotiate paid social usage rights upfront (not as an afterthought)
3. Test creator content as dark posts on Meta and TikTok Ads Manager
4. Creator-style content typically outperforms studio-produced ads 2–4x in CTR

Always negotiate usage rights in the initial contract. It's dramatically harder and more expensive to negotiate after the fact.

## Influencer Program Playbooks

### Seeding Program (Start Here — Zero Budget)

1. Identify 50–100 nano/micro influencers in your niche
2. Send personalized DMs — no ask, just introduce yourself and offer product
3. Ship product with a thoughtful note and custom packaging
4. Monitor for organic posts — do not pressure or require posting
5. Follow up with those who post organically: they're now warm partners

### Always-On Ambassador Program

1. Identify top performers from seeding or one-off campaigns
2. Offer a quarterly retainer: 2–4 posts/month for a fixed fee + commission
3. Give them an exclusive audience discount code (tracks their revenue impact)
4. Include in product feedback loops, early feature access, roadmap calls
5. Feature them on your website and social media as named ambassadors

### Performance / Affiliate Program (Scale)

1. Set up affiliate tracking (Rewardful, PartnerStack, Impact.com)
2. Recruit influencers as affiliates — zero upfront cost, pay on results
3. Provide creative asset packs and approved talking points
4. Offer 20–40% commission on first purchase
5. Tier payouts to reward top performers and incentivize growth

## Legal and Compliance

- **FTC Disclosure (US)**: All paid partnerships must disclose clearly. "#ad" or "Paid partnership" at the beginning of content — not buried at the end.
- **ASA Rules (UK)**: Similar disclosure requirements. "Ad" must be clearly visible.
- **GDPR (EU)**: Ensure audience data collected via campaigns complies with local regulations.
- **Contracts**: Always have a written agreement covering: deliverables, timeline, payment terms, usage rights, exclusivity window, approval process, FTC compliance requirements.
